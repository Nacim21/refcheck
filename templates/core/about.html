About RefCheck AI

RefCheck AI is designed as a lightweight assistant for reviewing basketball calls from short game clips.

How the system will work

  • Django templates for the web app
  • Google Cloud Storage for uploaded videos
  • Gemini for multimodal video analysis
  • Basketball rulebook retrieval for rule-grounded reasoning

Limitations

  • Basketball only for MVP
  • Short clips only
  • Depends on video angle and quality
  • Not an official referee review system
